How a paper gets on this list

Every Hacker News comment and story since 2007 that links an arXiv paper (or its alphaXiv, ar5iv or Hugging Face page) is counted. A paper is listed when at least 3 different people linked it, in at least 3 separate months, and a month counts only when someone who had not linked it before did, so one person posting the same paper every month counts once. That is Andrej Karpathy's rule for HackerTube with one change, made because a single account wrote half or more of the links for about one in ten papers that passed the original.

A link is not a vote. People link a paper to build on it, to make a point in an argument, or to disagree with it, and all three count the same here.

Hidden gems are lasting papers cited fewer than {{ gem_max }} times by other papers (Semantic Scholar's count), at least {{ gem_age }} years after they appeared: what HN keeps reading that research rarely cites.
