How this is measured

The {{ (snap.papers or 0) | commafy }} papers on Hugging Face's Daily Papers over the {{ snap.weeks }} weeks from {{ since }} are grouped into topics by meaning (Ward clustering of their BGE-M3 embeddings), and each topic's share of every week's list is followed. "Share at start" and "share at end" average the first and last four weeks.

A topic is called rising or cooling only if the slope of its weekly share beats the same slope with every paper's week shuffled 2,000 times, after a Benjamini–Hochberg correction for testing many topics at once (q below {{ snap.q_max }}). Shares, not counts, because the lists ran from about 100 to 400 papers a week. A topic is named by a phrase at least 30% of its papers use; no language model writes the names or the sentences.

Topic names can change from month to month as the grouping is redone, so compare months by the papers as well as the names.
