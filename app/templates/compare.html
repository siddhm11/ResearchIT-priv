Where ResearchIT falls short

  • No email digest yet: you have to come to the site.
  • arXiv only. Fields that publish elsewhere are better served by Semantic Scholar or Google Scholar.
  • No PDF reader, notes or citation graph.
  • No app; it is a web page that works on a phone.

What only ResearchIT has here

  • Topic trends in Hugging Face's Daily Papers that are called rising or cooling only when a statistical test says so.
  • Lasting: the arXiv papers Hacker News keeps linking, month after month, since 2007.
  • A feed that keeps a share of the page for each separate interest, so a side topic is not drowned out.

Papers with Code, often listed in older round-ups, closed in July 2025; its address now leads to Hugging Face's trending papers.
